# Solar Demand Aggregation ("Groupon of Solar") — strategic position note
DRAFT 2026-10-02 · From Triage ACT-007 START · Decision row ACT-010 (pending Ankit)

## The idea
Pool small module orders (e.g. 500 kW from installers, MSMEs, rooftop EPCs) into MW-scale bulk orders placed with a contracted supplier (SAEL, Grew Solar or others). Fixed calendar: orders close 30 days before each delivery; deliveries on the 15th and the last day of every month.

## Why it fits the North Star
- Revenue soon (KR1.1/1.2): commission on every pooled MW, paid by the supplier under a consultancy agreement.
- Networks (KR2.1/2.2): every cycle adds buyers; volume makes us a priority channel for suppliers.
- Trust (O3): one fixed calendar, written terms, dated promises.

## Positions we take
1. We are the aggregator and consultant, not the trader: supplier invoices buyers directly; we earn commission (above the 2% norm). No inventory, no title risk.
2. Money terms before work: signed supplier consultancy agreement before the first cycle; buyers confirm with an advance paid to the supplier.
3. Fixed calendar, no exceptions: cut-off 30 days before delivery; missed cut-off rolls to the next slot.
4. Minimum pool to trigger a cycle: [__] MW; below it, buyers are offered the next slot or a refund of the advance.
5. Standard SKUs only (2–3 module types per cycle) to get the bulk price.
6. Transparent pricing: one published price per slot per SKU.

## Positions we won't take
- Holding stock, taking delivery risk or extending credit to buyers.
- Custom specs or one-off delivery dates inside a pool.
- Promising a price before the supplier confirms the slot.
- Working with any supplier without a signed agreement and non-circumvention.
- Small buyers outside the pool on non-standard terms (small deals only under signed mandate and above-norm commission).
